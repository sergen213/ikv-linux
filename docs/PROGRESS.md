## 2026-10-09 user playtest: completion claim retracted

A real user session on the dev stack showed the client does **not** resemble the original game: the Source0 spawn `[0,0]` sits on the sea, the camera ends up among dark geometry with no visible player, untextured helper boxes are drawn, the scene is far darker than daylight reference footage, and the screen is covered by debug HUD buttons and diagnostics (`analysis/user-report-1/gl-wayland.png`, `gl-top.png`). The launcher was reported as too complicated. Reference: original-game gameplay VOD frames under `analysis/reference-video/` (local only, not redistributed). Criteria 2, 3, 4 and 6 below are therefore **not met** regardless of the earlier scoped proofs; remediation (spawn/camera/helper geometry/lighting, original-style HUD, simplified launcher) is in progress. Earlier passes proved narrower properties than visual/playable parity.

**Remediation landed (same day):** root cause of the "overlapping" world was inverted front-face winding (GL_CCW / VK CCW vs D3D-clockwise source triangles): exterior surfaces were culled, so interiors appeared as gray boxes and actors inside-out. Now GL_CW / VK clockwise. Daylight environment values (ambient 1.0, directional 100/255, lightmap 80/255, sky-coloured fog 300..1200) replace the constructor baseline; they are **NONCANONICAL**, tuned against the reference VOD because the live environment record is unrecovered. Spawn moved to the Yeni Cami courtyard `[386000,806000]`, gameplay distances rescaled to the 1000-units-per-metre visual scale, camera default pitch .55/distance 22 with a nocamera-collider occlusion clamp. HUD rebuilt from the original UI1/UI2 atlases (player/target frames, Mesajlaşma chat, action bar 1..0, toggled windows; diagnostics to stderr). Launcher reduced to install → login → character → OYNA with a settings dialog. Real RX7900XTX GL/Vulkan captures `analysis/user-report-1/after-{opengl,vulkan}.png`; FULL/GL-only/SAN 14/14 each (42/42). Remaining visible gaps vs reference: colder/greyer palette than the warm sunlit original, no minimap, no dynamic shadows of actors, particle member absent.

**Second playtest fixes:** (1) Questgiver and merchant shared `source_uid` 1001 (Agah); merchant is now 1002 Agop, and the server fills per-kind defaults once at load (enemy 16001/9, merchant 1002/17, questgiver 1001/16). The 373 classes.dat npc/monster records are listed in `analysis/user-report-2/npcmodels/npc-records.json`; 1004/1008/1013/1015 fail to assemble because of original-data biped or binding mismatches. ### Wind-driven sea

User report: water bodies did not react to the wind; later, with the reference of rolling Gerstner swells: the sea must build large waves in strong wind, and foam where those waves hit something. Root cause: the wave-27 wind response was too weak to read. The only geometry change was the original 1D `v_ocean` swell (50 m wavelength, ±0.12 m) scaled up to ×2.5. The ocean meshes are coarse (`ocean_ada_*` mean edges 26–130 units, 6–32 m), so they could not carry real wind waves. The shaded sea kept its pattern and only grew brighter with wind; still air was never calmer than 0.13.0's default sea. All new behaviour is NONCANONICAL and enhanced-appearance-only; with inactive weather (`weatherMisc.w == 0`) and in the original appearance the source ocean mesh and the shaders take the exact pre-wave paths.

- **Wind sea (`oceanWindWaves`: Vulkan `frame.glsl`, identical GL copy `IKV_GL_OCEAN_WIND` in `renderer_gl.cpp`):** summed Gerstner waves spread around the wind (60/42/30 m used; 21/15 m defined). Their height grows with the square of the wind: the longest is ~1.9 m high (crest to trough ~3.8 m, more where waves add up) in a 22 m/s gusting gale, ~0.3 m at 10 m/s, and nothing in still air. Steepness rises with the wind and pulls the water toward the crests (sharp crests, flat troughs, no looping). The waves travel downwind with the wind phase `wind.w` (7/9/11 crests per 1024 m wrap, so the wrap causes no jump) and fade out with eye distance by 8 wavelengths. The vertices and the shading use the same three waves; normals come from the analytic Gerstner slope, and whitecaps break where the Gerstner Jacobian compresses the crests. The source swell runs from ×0.6 in still air to ~×2.8 in a gale (was ×1 → ×2.5).
- **Ocean mesh (`renderer.hpp` `subdivide_ocean_mesh`, `ocean_wave_mesh`, `ocean_bed`; GL and Vulkan uploads):** enhanced uploads every source-ocean mesh twice: the source mesh and a copy split by conforming longest-edge bisection to ≤ 24-unit edges (≤ 400k triangles per mesh; Sivri Ada 40k → ~650k). The copy draws only while the wind sea exists (weather active, wind above 2 m/s, off below 1.5 m/s) and only for placements within 1920 units of the eye; otherwise the source mesh draws. At either switch the wind sea is a few centimetres high or faded out.
- **Shoaling:** a top-down bed grid of all static non-ocean geometry gives each vertex of the copy a shore factor (second UV). Waves shrink over the last 4 m of water depth and over the last 50 m before land (distance transform of the grid), so swells die out before shores, rocks, piers and hulls instead of rising through them or above the far beach in deep channels. The shading fades the same way by the depth behind each pixel.
- **Foam (`water.glsl`):** contact foam where the water meets anything in front of the bed (shore, rocks, piers, hulls, characters standing in water): a depth-buffer band behind the surface, ~8 cm in still air to ~1 m in a gale, wider under crests, laced and moving; whitecaps on compressed crests from ~6 m/s; the shore surf spreads over the shallows with a soft depth fade. The wave-27 downwind foam streaks are gone (they read as straight diagonal light bands on gale wave faces).
- **Wall surf (`renderer.hpp` `ocean_shore_field`; GL unit 10 / Vulkan binding 15, `waterShoreField`):** quay walls got no foam: their sea faces turn away from the eye, so the depth-buffer band never sees them, and Eminönü has no modelled sea floor. The bed grid's distance to land and the bed's depth are now an RG8 texture (one texel per grid cell, 64-unit range) that the water samples per pixel, so the band hugs the wall whatever the ocean mesh's density. Only land rising out of water at least ~1 m deep and steeper than ~1 in 3 (bed depth over distance to land, both from the grid: quay and pier walls, rocks, hulls) throws it; beaches and the shallow Sivri Ada lagoon keep their surf. Waves breaking on it throw foam back out to sea: solid at the wall, laced further out, surging with each incoming crest in moving tongues, ~1.5–4.5 m wide at ~3/4 strength at 10 m/s, ~3–10 m at full strength in a gale, none in still air. An eye above the Eminönü quay sees the water only from ~3.5 m past its edge, so the band has to reach past that. Water more than 15 m from land (most of the open sea) skips the band's work.
- **Refraction behind characters:** a column of deep blue-green sea ran up the surf above the player on the Sivri Ada beach in a gale (GL and Vulkan). Debug views of the trace showed the cause: the refracted rays of the pixels above the player cross the screen down toward their vanishing point, which the camera puts at the player, and pass behind the character; the bed they reach is hidden and the hit was rejected (it lies far behind the surface found). Those pixels then fell back to the normal-offset refraction, which measures the water along the view ray: at grazing views far more water than the refracted rays around them cross. With weather active, such an occluded ray now crosses the bed grid's heightfield in world space (the same texture: 12 quadratic steps and 4 bisections), so its water path and depth match its traced neighbours; the colour is the bed behind the pixel, and the path is at least the refracted ray's to the screen depth behind the pixel. Rejecting hits on the occluder (the first attempt) did not help: the bed hits on sloping shallows lie behind the bed in front of them too, so it rejected valid hits across the near surf. Open: from the near-shore camera at 10 m/s the shallow Sivri Ada lagoon reads paler and whiter than on main (green in calm and gale); not wall foam (debug), not the estimate's path length (the minimum path left it unchanged); the cause is not yet isolated (`analysis/porting-wave28/Sea/sea5/sheet-lagoon.png`).
- **Shading (`water.glsl`):** still air gives a near-glassy sea (chop ×0.2, afl waves ×0.25, sharper glint, thinner shore lace); with wind the small waves lengthen ×1.4, stretch ×2.2 across the wind and steepen. Everything is continuous in wind speed and gust; no new passes.
- **Performance:** Vulkan water draws use their own pipelines (`water_pipeline_`/`water_double_`: the scene fragment shader with specialisation constant `kWaterPipeline`), so the rest of the scene no longer carries the water/wind-sea/foam code. Measured against 0.13.0 (9f4f037), 600 ticks, uncapped GPU timing, 1 run each (`analysis/porting-wave28/Metrics/perf-sea3-vs-base.json`; before the split, `perf-main-dfc1674-vs-base.json`): Vulkan enhanced scene pass, Sivri Ada calm 0.400 → 0.401 ms (was 0.460 before the split), the server's own weather 0.405 → 0.499 ms (was 0.540), 22 m/s gale 0.426 → 0.606 ms (was 0.665); Eminönü −5 to −6 % in every weather (was +5 to +11 %). OpenGL enhanced: Ada gale 0.557 → 0.731 ms (+31 %), the rest within +1 to +11 %. Vulkan RT faithful: −16 to −0 %. The remaining Ada cost is the wave mesh (~650k triangles) while the wind sea is on. Wall surf and the occluded-refraction march against main 210f3e0 in a 22 m/s gale (600 ticks, uncapped GPU timing, 1 run each, `analysis/porting-wave28/Sea/sea5/perf-sea5-vs-main.json`): Sivri Ada Vulkan scene pass 0.604 → 0.630 ms (+4.2 %, frame total +2.3 %), OpenGL total 0.731 → 0.747 ms (+2.2 %); Eminönü Vulkan scene pass 0.292 → 0.315 ms (+8.0 %, frame total +1.9 %; +12.5 % before open water skipped the wall surf), OpenGL 0.474 → 0.503 ms (+5.9 %).
- Evidence: `analysis/porting-wave28/Sea/` (`gerstner/` and `sea3/` GL+Vulkan sheets: Sivri Ada default and near-shore cameras and the Eminönü quay at calm / 10 m/s / 22 m/s; `sheet-shore-character.png` a character at the Ada waterline in a gale; `sea5/` the column before/after against main, the quay wall surf at calm / 10 m/s / 22 m/s, refraction elsewhere, `perf-sea5-vs-main.json`), `analysis/porting-wave28/Metrics/` (A/B against 9f4f037).
