# Independent server architecture

## Authenticated, persistent native world

The native C++20 world service uses the custom v2 codec, TLS and PostgreSQL-backed account/session/character ownership. The HTTPS authentication/management service and world process are separate native executables sharing the `Store` implementation. This is an independent community server, **not a recovered original İKV backend**. Original observations in [asset evidence](ASSET_FORMATS.md#documented-version-6-gameplay-not-backend-formulas) and [network evidence](network/PACKET_CATALOG.md) are distinct from [the custom wire](network/PROTOCOL_SPECIFICATION.md).

```text
Qt launcher / HTTPS management -> ikv-auth -> PostgreSQL
SDL3 native client / TLS       -> ikv-server -> World -> Store -> PostgreSQL
                                      |          |
                              session ownership  fixed 20Hz / spatial AOI
```

`World` owns gameplay state, movement, interactions and social state. `Store` owns SQL transactions, sessions, leases and durable guild membership. Public account APIs do not authorize client-supplied gameplay outcomes. Authenticated entry includes account ID, character ID, name, integer class ID (1 warrior, 2 mage, 3 healer), appearance and saved state. A character cannot concurrently enter twice. Snapshot player IDs occupy `1..0x7fffffff`; configured NPC IDs occupy the upper uint32 namespace. Transport IDs are not durable character IDs.

## Configured world and source provenance

[`config/gameplay.json`](../config/gameplay.json) is explicitly **NONCANONICAL** as a whole, but its world content is generated from the original tables by `tools/generate_content.cpp` (target `ikv-content-generator`, run as `ikv-content-generator original/app/base config/gameplay.json [extract.json]`). The generator decodes `data/classes.dat`, `animation.dat` and `prefixes.dat` with the shared `load_source_records` decoder, loads the zone support grids, rewrites only `provenance`, `defaults`, `zones`, `items`, `quests` and `npcs`, keeps every other top-level key, and validates the result by constructing `World` with the real grids before writing. The optional extract file lists all 373 classes.dat actors, 681 weapons/armour/potions and 421 `<loc>` records in the native schema (current copy: `analysis/porting-wave2/Content/original_content.json`).

Original per entry (`source_uid` is the classes.dat uid): NPC/monster names, model UIDs/classes, levels, attack rate/range, aggro (`triggerrange`), XP, dropped money, run/walk speed (animation.dat `forwardspeed` × 50 wire/tick); weapon/armour names, icons, slots, class/sex limits (enforced on equip), attack speed/range and prices; potion names and heal/Kudret amounts (İksir prototype 14001 + index-3 prefixes). HP, damage and potion amounts are scaled by 100/383 (VOD 18:30: a level-2 Büyücü has Enerji 383; the native default is 100 HP). Armour is a NONCANONICAL flat `dmgreduction`×200.

**Zones.** All 15 open-world registry scenes are configured: `eminonu`0, `misir`1, `antrepo`2, `labirent`3, `meteor`4, `ada`5, `savas`6 (Er Meydanı 1, no inbound door), `yeralti`26, `migrat`36, `siginak`46, `cemberli`66, `topkapi`67, `buyukhol`68, `karakoy`69, `karakoyic`70. The numbered instance copies (Er Meydanı 2-20, Migrat/Sığınaklar 1-9, Çemberlitaş 1-19) are not configured. Each zone has `triggers`: original `<loc>` door/crystal centres (`areacenter` x/z × 1000). Walking into a trigger moves the player to its `arrive` point in `to` (`advance()`, after movement). The pairing of doors (from names such as `eminonumisirkapi1`/`misirkapi1`, `eminonu_to_topkapi_1`/`topkapi_to_eminonu_1`) is an inference, because destinations were server data. The walk-in radius is the smallest radius up to `arearadius` (capped at 4 units) that reaches walkable support. The original 20-50 unit `arearadius` overlaps neighbouring Topkapı crystals, so it was an interaction distance. `requiredquestid` is kept as `source_required_quest` but is not enforced, because the quest chains are server-only. The generator places each arrival outside every trigger of its zone, and validation rejects any arrival or spawn inside a trigger, so a transfer can never bounce back. The typed `{"action":"zone"}` is an administrator debug jump to a zone spawn. The current table yields 54 triggers.

**Population (NONCANONICAL).** Eminönü has Agah Efendi (quest `first_hunt`: kill one original Fare `m16001`), Agop (level 1-5 weapons), İşportacı Genç and Ayşe Teyze (potions), Halime Teyze (armour), Jandarma, and Bankacı. Bankacı stands at the `Bankacı` mark of `ui/zone00.dds`; a minimap pixel equals 2048 minus the support cell. In Mısır Çarşısı, Şevket Bey (aktar), Dilek (demirci) and Fahri Bey (terzi) stand at the `Aktar`/`Demirci`/`Terzi` marks of `ui/zone01.dds`, and Elif sells high-level potions. There are also merchants in Topkapı and Karaköy. Monster groups are three families of three per zone, chosen by a level band per zone (Eminönü 1-5 … Karaköy İç 50-60). They stand on the spawn's connected walkable area, away from triggers. The generator first assembles every model with `attach_source_npc` and excludes models that fail with "BIP absent". Bankacı carries the `banker` flag that enables the `bank_*` actions ([custom protocol](network/CUSTOM_PROTOCOL.md)).

Validation rejects unknown references, malformed/bounded records, obstructed spawns, invalid triggers, unsupported skill/objective types and oversize catalogs. The limits are 32 zones, 64 triggers per zone, 1,024 NPCs, 1,024 items, 64 quests, 64 skills, 1,024 recipes and 2,048 prefixes. Each actor state is limited to 8,192 encoded bytes. State frames always carry own state, catalogs, group members and every critical event; other players (nearest first), then NPC entities (nearest first, with the shop/loot item definitions they introduce) fill the remaining frame budget. The per-player item catalog holds only inventory, quest-reward, learned-recipe, open-bank and visible shop/loot items. At admission, a saved zone or item that the regenerated content no longer defines falls back to the default spawn or is dropped.

## Tick, collision and interest

`advance()` advances one 50 ms tick; wall-clock scheduling belongs to the server loop. Provisional cardinal input and ground-click destinations share sequence ordering. Movement is `movement_step` coordinate units per tick (800 in the supplied config), scaled by speed buffs; stuns stop it. Destinations are planned by `route()`: a direct clear segment, or in source zones a bounded A* (1,000 popped nodes) over the 2,048² support grid; an unroutable destination queues a `blocked_destination` correction. Moves must remain within configured zone bounds and avoid rectangles, including thin walls crossed between endpoints. Blocked movement cancels intent and queues a `collision` correction containing the last authoritative coordinates. Death and moderator kicks stop movement.

A cell index keyed by `(zone, floor(x/cell_size), floor(y/cell_size))` tracks players and NPCs. Nearby cells are queried and exact squared distance filters AOI; both snapshots and entity replication exclude other zones and distant actors. Snapshot entries are ID-sorted; state entities are ordered group members first, then nearest first. This is one authoritative world process with a 32-player cap, not a measured MMO-scale capacity claim. Client rendering/interpolation does not grant movement authority.

`World::set_source_zones` installs the recovered original 2,048² support grids before admission; for a zone with `source_registry_id`, `passable()` additionally requires a supported grid cell. Unsupported cells fail closed. Installation rejects any configured spawn, trigger arrival, default spawn, NPC position or patrol leg without a supported bounded route. In source zones `clear_path` samples each segment at intervals of at most 750 integer units (half the recovered 1.5-unit cell) and rejects diagonal corner cuts. The server owns explicit asset-root/zone/collision-zone selection and coordinate conversion; other configured zones remain independent. Merely loading original scene assets does not make independent NPC placements or gameplay definitions original map records.

## State and actions

The exact `state` object, every action name with its fields and rejection codes, every event type and the limits are in [custom protocol](network/CUSTOM_PROTOCOL.md); this section only summarizes the server side.

`World::state(id)` builds the self state and drains the actor's event queue. The server loop omits `catalogs` when they are byte-identical to the copy last sent on that connection; the native client keeps the last accepted copy. This keeps a client that pauses reading for several seconds (scene load, first GPU upload) under the 256 KiB `output_limit`.

Each event queue is bounded to 32 events and 32,768 encoded event bytes. Superseded trade terms are coalesced, and overflow discards older noncritical events before current trade/kick/terminal notices. State packing measures the exact remaining 65,533-byte JSON frame budget and reserves every queued event before optional entities (events are delivered once, entities every frame), so nearby players and NPCs drop out of a crowded frame before any notice does; only if group members alone overflow are older noncritical notices omitted, never current critical consent/kick events. Configuration admission validates a worst-case state with maximum own state, stats and catalogs, five other group members, a complete 32+32-item exchange and 31 critical notices; other players and NPCs are admitted per frame within the remaining budget. Item IDs are bounded to 34 encoded JSON bytes, item names to 64 encoded JSON bytes, character names to 32 UTF-8 bytes, and item UIDs are exactly 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters. A kicked actor receives the final `kick` event and subsequent events cannot bury it. Successful economy/gameplay actions acknowledge only after persistence. Private text is never copied into administrative action logs.

Malformed requests and permission/range/alive/resource/state/ownership/quantity violations queue `error` events rather than writing state. World actions do not accept arbitrary state replacement. Bounds and limits are independent defensive/gameplay rules, not recovered original constants.

## Gameplay transitions

Enemies patrol configured waypoints, acquire nearby living players, accumulate threat from damage/healing, chase, attack at configured cooldowns, leash home and respawn. NPCs whose classes.dat record has `idlewalktend` follow it instead of a patrol (`World::idle_walk`, NONCANONICAL timing: a percent roll every 5 s, a walk at `walk_speed` to a reachable point within 12 units of the spawn, a 60 s stand after serving a player or while someone's bank is open; 0 stands). Leashing clears threat and ownership and restores health. First successful damage claims XP/loot. Claimant death permits a later attacker to claim; death/disconnect removes stale NPC targeting. Nearby living group members share kill XP/objectives, and group loot is independently randomly assigned among those members. NPC HP, corpse claims and respawn are world-process state; character outcomes are persistent.

Basic attacks deal `attack_damage` plus attack buffs, worn weapon damage and Efsun attack attributes; a critical roll (weapon crit plus Efsun chance) multiplies by the weapon's crit multiplier (default 2), then power buffs scale the result. Weapon `attack_range`/`attack_cooldown` apply, bare hands use `combat_range`/`attack_cooldown`. NPC `vulnerable` debuffs raise damage taken; hits on players subtract the victim's buff and gear armour (minimum 1). Skills enforce class/level/range, mana and cooldown; configured damage/healing/armor buffs/healer revival are applied authoritatively. Buffs expire on fixed ticks and are cleared on reconnect because process tick epochs do not survive restart. Death clears buffs, stops movement, charges `death_currency_percent` of currency, wears equipment (`item_rules.death_wear_permille`), ends Kabadayı mode and queues death. Resurrection after `death_resurrect_delay` ticks restores HP/mana at the current zone's spawn. There is no invented death-XP penalty. Quest kills advance accepted unfinished objectives; completion validates the correct giver/objective, prevents repeat rewards and commits configured XP/currency/items. XP changes advance levels and configured resource maxima; rewards diminish above the configured quest/enemy level band.

### Skills (original skills.dat set)

`config/gameplay.json` `skills` holds the 43 original class skills generated by `analysis/porting-wave1/Skills/generate_skills.py` from encrypted `data/skills.dat` (dump: `analysis/porting-wave1/Skills/skills.json`): 15 Savaşçı (classindex 0), 14 Büyücü, 14 Şifacı. Each entry keeps `source:[classindex,skillno]` (class_id = classindex+1; the original wire skill id is `classindex*15+skillno`, exe `0044ccf0`/`0044cfe0`/`004a6a80`), the original Turkish `name`, the `level` requirement, `target` (`self|enemy|ally|enemies|allies|passive`) and base `cooldown`/`duration` (ticks) parsed from the original detail text (`Seviye Gereksinimi`, `Hedef`, `Süre`, `Yenilenme`). Zihin Saldırısı keeps the detail's ×3 duration against creatures. Mana, range/radius and effect magnitudes are **NONCANONICAL** (they lived on the official server). The original client's 0x48-byte skill record (`0044dad0`) holds only name/detail/sound/femalesound/hasicon/icon.

Characters store invested points as `state.skills{id:rank}`. `skill_learn{skills:{id:points}}` is the Yetenekler ✓ batch (original 0044d4e0 sends every pending point at once): class, level, variant slot, the 15-point cap (detail text "15 puanda") per skill and the budget `skill_points_base + skill_points_per_level*(level-1)` (one point per level per the v6 guide; base 1) over the whole batch are enforced, all-or-nothing; unspent points are published as top-level `skill_points`. `skill` requires rank ≥ 1, rejects passives, enforces required stances (`requires`: Sert/Ağır Vuruş need Ofansif Dövüş, Kışkırtma/Sakınma/Savaş Narası/Hedef Saptırma need Defansif Dövüşme), Gazap's heal block, Sarsılmaz's no-action rule, mana, the per-skill cooldown and a 20-tick global cooldown (original client `0044cfb0` applies 1000 ms to every slot). Effects: single/area damage; spells (a `school`) roll Büyü Kritik (1 % + Efsun 37, ×2 + Efsun 38, client 00402f60) plus detail-text `crit`/`crit_vulnerable` and apply `on_hit`/`on_crit` riders (Buz Oku slow/stun, Meteorit armor_break); NPC and harmful player effects with expiry (stun, `slow`, `vulnerable`, `armor_break`, damage-over-time and Kanatma `drain` every 60 ticks credited to the living caster — "3 saniyede bir"); taunt; heal/revive; buffs `{id,skill_source,expires,armor,attack,speed,regen,hot,power,absorb,immune,wrath,stance}`; stances are mutually exclusive and recasting one closes it; Hedef Saptırma moves an ally's NPC threat to the caster; Can Kurtaran absorbs one hit above 5% of maximum health; Meditasyon regenerates mana each second and İyileştirme Çemberi heals every 3 s; Zihin Toplama / Büyü Bozma remove harmful effects of their kind (`cleanse` = caster classindex: Savaşsal/Büyüsel/Ruhsal, [INFERENCE]) and, on the `cleanse_all` roll, all kinds. Summons Boz Ayı/Tesla Küresi/Çağrı fight hostile NPCs and players hostile to their caster. Ranks scale magnitudes by `skill_rank_percent` per extra point and learned Bilgi passives boost their school by `passive_rank_percent` per point (both NONCANONICAL). Movement uses the summed buff `speed` percentage (Depar +50%, Konsantrasyon/Sarsılmaz root); stunned NPCs neither move nor attack. Events: `skill{source,target,skill}` per cast (single-target damage adds `damage/hp/owner/critical?`), `hit{source,target,skill,damage,hp,critical?}` per area/over-time hit, `combat` for auto-attacks and hits on players.

Health/mana growth is bounded by the configured `resource_cap` (1,000,000 in the supplied independent definitions), including saved-state admission, so a valid leveled character remains valid after reconnect.

Stacks obey per-item limits and bag capacity. Item UIDs are random 128-bit identifiers generated through OpenSSL; equipment references owned stacks rather than creating extra items. Splitting produces a distinct owned stack and consumes space. Healing consumables consume one item and cannot be wasted at full health. Merchant interactions require a living nearby merchant and validated catalog. Regular vendor return uses the guide's documented **1/5** ratio. The documented **1/3 Plus** ratio is retained in configuration but is not granted from an untrusted or invented entitlement; no Plus entitlement exists in this independent account system.

Equipment admission and every candidate save enforce eight occupied slots. Equipping a new distinct slot when eight are occupied is rejected before persistence; replacing an existing occupied slot is allowed. Database failure leaves the existing equipment/inventory unchanged, and every successfully saved equipment map remains admissible on reconnect.

## Economy commit and failure semantics

World constructs candidate character states without changing live caches, checks them, and calls the persistence callback before applying outcomes or acknowledging them. Purchases, sales, quest rewards, loot, split/equip/use, player combat outcomes and guild mutations use that commit point. Persistence exceptions become action `persistence` errors with unchanged balances, items, HP, NPC claims and cooldown acknowledgements. Tick-driven enemy attacks also commit before changing target resources. Explicit `save_all`/disconnect saves may propagate persistence errors to the server's save/lease error handling rather than report a false save.

Disconnect save failure retains the actor cache and propagates the failure; the server stops its authority loop instead of continuing to serve a disconnected ghost. Its final save can retry and release leases on success; unreleased leases expire through the existing heartbeat policy.

Exchange has two distinct consent stages: sender requests, recipient accepts the invitation to open the exchange, then both confirm their current offers. Every request and offer edit receives a globally fresh uint64 revision within the current world process; overflow is rejected rather than wrapped. Both invitation acceptance and final confirmation require the exact displayed current `trade_accept{revision}`. Missing, stale or previous-exchange revisions queue `trade_revision` errors without changing consent. Every offer edit invalidates **both** confirmations.

The authoritative event is `{type:"trade",peer,peer_name,initiator,phase:"invited"|"open",revision,own:{items:[{uid,item,name,quantity}],currency,accepted},other:{items:[{uid,item,name,quantity}],currency,accepted}}`. Each item's definition/name comes from the offering character's owned UID and server catalog, never a client label or peer inventory replication. Both parties receive complete own/other terms and both confirmation flags. The request emits the full invited event to both parties, then recipient-only `trade_invite{from,phase:"invited",revision}`. Only the recipient (`self != initiator`) may accept an invitation; offers are accepted only in the open phase. A full 32+32-item exchange is supported without truncating its authoritative terms. The client presents this trusted peer identity and the latest complete terms before enabling revision-bound confirmation.

Finalization revalidates both parties' alive/range/ownership/quantities/currency, removes both outgoing item sets before fitting incoming items, and persists **both characters in one callback/SQL transaction**. The final confirmer's flag is not changed before that transaction succeeds. Database failure leaves both character caches and the previous phase/revision/confirmation flags unchanged. It refreshes authoritative current terms for both parties, then queues the attempted confirmer `{type:"error",code:"trade_persistence",peer,revision,message}`; an explicit retry requires fresh presentation and the same current revision. Final success emits `trade_complete{peer}` without resolving consumed outgoing UIDs. Moving apart, death, zone changes, kicks or disconnect emits `trade_cancel{}` and cancels exchange. Inventory use/equipment/vendor mutations are blocked during exchange.

## Chat, groups, guilds and moderation

Chat channels are `local` and `roleplay` (AOI), `zone`, `private` (online target), `group`, `guild` and `clan` (membership required). A `local`/`roleplay` message starting with `/` plays a configured emote instead. Chat is bounded to 512 bytes, excludes control characters, is limited to one message per ten ticks and is not delivered to listeners who ignore the speaker. Groups are transient, require leader invitations/acceptance, cap membership at six, elect a successor when the leader leaves, and remove membership on disconnect. Group objectives and kill XP share only among nearby living members.

The guide additionally documents guild chat ([evidence](ASSET_FORMATS.md#contradictions-and-exact-limits)), so guilds are durable rather than an empty planned feature. Independent guild organization rules are explicitly NONCANONICAL: maximum 64 members, leader invitations/online kicks, and first remaining member as successor. World writes the same full revised roster into affected online character states in one batch. Store maintains its transactional guild registry/membership tables and overlays authoritative membership on offline character reads/reconnects, including stale former-leader records. Leaving the final member writes a revisioned empty-roster tombstone before clearing live membership. Guild membership is not abandoned merely by disconnecting.

Only administrative actors may mute/unmute/kick online players. In-world moderation and external persistent administrative mutes are separate flags; external refresh cannot cancel a local mute. `moderation(id,bool)` updates the external flag from the authenticated management record. A kick freezes the actor and emits `{type:"kick",by}` for the server to close that transport. `status()` supplies real player/NPC/zone/group/trade counts and a bounded 64-entry gameplay log of accepted actions/rejection codes, without chat bodies, credentials or tokens.

## Server-side bots (NONCANONICAL)

The original game had no bots. `src/world_bots.cpp` adds peerless actors (`World::add_bot`, ids `0x70000000+`) that act only through `handle()`/`destination()`, so every range, cooldown, mana, ownership, door and quest rule applies; they never take one of the 32 player slots. Behaviours (`config/gameplay.json` `bots`): `town` (wander near the zone spawn, `/otur`, emotes, canned Yerel chat), `hunt` (level-fitting monsters reached through doors, class skills, Ganimet, rest, örs repair, merchant selling/potions, quests), `companion` (accepts a player's `group_invite`, heals as Şifacı, follows through the same doors, assists the leader's target, leaves on `/dismiss`/`/ayrıl` in group or whisper, Gruptan Çıkar or leader logout). A bot new to its zone (freshly created, or saved in another zone) is admitted at a random spot within the town wander radius (`aoi`/4) that `clear_path()` reaches straight from the zone spawn (the spawn after 16 misses); a position saved in the zone is resumed, and admission validates either like a login. First thinks are staggered over `think_ticks`. Characters belong to the reserved non-loginable `ikv_bots` account (`Store::bot_account`), hold normal leases and persist via `save_all()` (32-character batches) and `remove()`; bot-only commits skip the per-action database write. `ikv-server --bots N` overrides the configured population; `BotHost` (`src/bots.cpp`) serves admin `bots_spawn`/`bots_despawn` and the world publishes `status.bots`. Snapshot and state list self plus at most 31 nearby players. Measured: 200 bots on the original Eminönü/Mısır grids, Release server: 6.1 % of one core, 19.7 ticks/s, tick_ms median 1.4 / p95 15.8 / max 24.3 (`analysis/porting-wave6/Bots/live1/evidence.json`). Start placement before/after: `analysis/porting-wave9/BotSpawn/` (fresh start, 1 s after READY: 6 → 20 distinct positions of 20 Eminönü bots, 15 → 0 within 3 units of the spawn).
