# Known issues and uncertainty register

## Confidence vocabulary

- **SOURCE-CONFIRMED**: concrete inspected independent implementation; not execution success.
- **STATIC-CONFIRMED**: original bytes or address-linked Ghidra/data/control flow; applies only to the documented path.
- **OBSERVED-RUNTIME**: exact parent-exercised command/outcome/artifact; never inferred from installed libraries, code or feature enumeration.
- **NONCANONICAL**: explicitly configured independent implementation where original semantics/formulas are unconfirmed.
- **UNKNOWN**: specific unrecovered semantics or unexercised acceptance. Missing evidence is not evidence the original lacks a feature.

## Current open items (2026-10-11, after waves 1–12)

- **Windows build (wave 17):** tested under Wine 11.19 only, never on a real Windows PC; unsigned executables (SmartScreen warning). `ikv-client.exe` imports `vulkan-1.dll`, so even OpenGL needs a Vulkan-capable GPU driver (any current AMD/NVIDIA/Intel driver ships it). No hosting and no self-update on Windows (install the next Setup.exe over it). A server's CA file is required (no Windows certificate-store trust). Command-line paths outside the user's ANSI code page do not round-trip. Frame generation under Windows not exercised. Mono ambient beds now play 3 dB louder on both platforms (in-process stb_vorbis replaced the ffmpeg `-ac 2` upmix).
- **LAN hosting (wave 17):** a host firewall can still block other PCs: on this machine ufw drops the auth/world ports until `sudo ufw allow 7445,7001/tcp`. A launcher built on a host with a different PostgreSQL major version than the one that created the hosted database cannot start it (the AppImage bundles PostgreSQL 16).
- **Interior lighting (wave 17, NONCANONICAL):** roofed interiors (drawn geometry over ≥99.5 % of sampled walk cells: Mısır Çarşısı, Antrepo, Labirent, Migrat, Sığınaklar, Büyük Hol, Karaköy İç Mekan incl. the Umut Dergisi room) use the noon record at every hour (`clock.interior_hour`); the original server's per-zone records are unknown. Partly open zones keep the day/night cycle and so stay dark at night: Yeraltı (55 % covered), Çemberlitaş (46 %), Sivri Ada (12 %), like the outdoor towns. Seen once in 80 v0.6.0 sweep runs: a Mısır → Eminönü instant-door frame at night with a translucent red full-screen tint (`analysis/porting-wave17/MisirLights/before-v060/misir_to_eminonu-vk-rt-h1-pair.png`); not reproduced in its rerun or in the 130 later runs, cause unattributed.
- **Doorways and doors (wave 16):** only 3 door pairs are see-through portals (Antrepo, Mısır Balıkpazarı gate, Umut Dergisi); the other doorway candidates (Büyük Hol Arz/Lodos gates, Haseki gate, Sığınak vault doors, Serum Oda, vents) are instant doors, because their two sides do not line up (Mısır's interior is ~1.14× the exterior: looking back from inside is exact only at the Balıkpazarı gate) or need a per-player destination (one meteor gate serves both Büyük Hol entrances). Portal thresholds, floors, heights and walkable aprons are NONCANONICAL fits to the drawn geometry. Door leaves have no opening animation and are hidden while their portal is open. Through a portal: no destination particles or hover contours; only the first portal on screen gets destination shadows in enhanced raster; light does not cross the opening in RT. Bots still teleport at doors. Instant doors stream only the nearest door's zone; admin/arena jumps, deaths and misses use the wave-15 fade (black hold ~0.7 s raster / ~1.0 s RT in Eminönü). Resident zones cost VRAM (RT: Eminönü ~1.85 GiB, Antrepo +0.18, Mısır +0.06). A ~15–25 ms presentation stall every 10.0 s is outside IKV (also seen with `vkcube` on this GNOME desktop). Zones are separate maps, so an open world without doors is out of reach of the original data.
- **Parties (wave 15):** groups are still in-memory only (lost on world restart). Cross-zone invites are NONCANONICAL. A party formed across instance copies is not merged until members re-enter through a door. Phasing is not implemented.

- **Vulkan foliage (wave 11, fixed, OBSERVED):** the reported leaf flicker came from the RT denoiser never accumulating blended (alpha-tested) draws while those draws wiped the guide behind them, and from FSR treating leaves as 0.9-reactive with no motion vectors. - **Physical RT decal rectangles (wave 13, fixed, OBSERVED):** alpha-tested placements were opaque quads in the RT acceleration structure, so the street under the paper-litter decals (`kirletme_a_`/`kirletme_b_`, e.g. `kirli_gazete_*`) lost its Sun ray and every GI/reflection probe and rendered black (also with `IKV_RT_DENOISE=0`); trees cast blob shadows. Decal view dark px (max<24) 139,934 → 51,516 (physical), 129,469 → 51,281 (physical + FSR3 + FG); faithful frames identical. Cost: physical GPU 8.19 → 10.95 ms (decal view) and 11.02 → 13.51 ms (courtyard, whose mosque wall/bush material is alpha-tested) at 1080p; faithful unchanged (1.60–2.56 → 1.80–2.57 ms, noise). Limits: at most 64 alpha textures per zone (zones use ≤ 22); a texture first met by an in-frame actor append stays an opaque quad until the next RT rebuild; the second-order Sun ray from a probe hit still sees opaque quads (`analysis/porting-wave13/PhysicalDecal/report.md`).
- **FSR 3.1.4 (Vulkan):** Ultra Performance (1/3 per axis) is soft and shows faint disocclusion ghosts at low frame rates; water/UV-scrolled materials have no texture-motion vectors (FSR relies on its own shading-change detection there). See [graphics](GRAPHICS_ROADMAP.md#fsr-314-temporal-upscaling-vulkan).
- **FSR 3.1.4 frame generation (Vulkan, opt-in):** adds about half a rendered-frame interval of latency; under FIFO the displayed cadence is quantized to whole refreshes (6.06/12.13 ms mix at 165 Hz when the rendered rate is not exactly half the refresh; no VRR). The HUD overlay (incl. name labels) of the newest frame is composited onto the generated frame, so world-anchored labels can lead the interpolated scene by half a frame. Fast motion at low rendered rates shows mild interpolation smear around limbs (no double images observed, `analysis/porting-wave10/FrameGen/crop-run-*.png`). Display-time measurement (`VK_EXT_present_timing`) is Wayland-only: on Xvfb Mesa 26.2's X11 present-timing path raised SIGFPE inside RADV, so X11 logs present-call cadence. The SDK's optical-flow/interpolation context creation has no release-on-failure path upstream; a failure there (not observed) would leak what it created before falling back to no FSR. See [graphics](GRAPHICS_ROADMAP.md#fsr-314-frame-generation-vulkan-ikv-owned-pacing).

- **RT frame hitches (wave 12, mostly addressed):** the attach/detach spikes of wave 9 (31–42 ms frames ending just before an actor attach) are addressed. - **Shared-GPU contention (wave 12, environment):** the largest measured source of RT dips is other processes on the same GPU. Above about 300 ms/s of foreign GPU time, RT frames sit at 28–45 ms for 10–30 s while IKV's own passes stay at 10–12 ms. RT needs about 12.4 ms of the 12.13 ms FG budget, so any sharing pushes it past the budget; raster modes (2–4 ms) are unaffected (`analysis/porting-wave12/Perf/report.md` #1). Requesting a high-priority queue (`VK_EXT_global_priority`) is untried [INFERENCE: needs CAP_SYS_NICE for HIGH on amdgpu].
